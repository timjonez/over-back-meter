    How this is counted

    Phrase counts, not sentiment. Irony, jokes, and people quoting the phrase all count. Retweets are excluded. The bare phrase “it's over” is not included.

    Score = round((back − over) / (back + over) × 100), from −100 (so over) to +100 (so back). At or below −20 the verdict is IT'S SO OVER. From −19 to +19 it is IT'S... MID. At or above +20 it is WE'RE SO BACK.

    The numbers are written by hand into data.json. This page does not call the X API. A new day is one appended entry, and the newest date becomes the reading above.

    The reading shipped with this page is a manual snapshot from November 26, 2026, 8:16 AM PT: over 902, back 2,042, score +39. November 23 is an earlier manual count: back 5,948, over 955, score +72.
